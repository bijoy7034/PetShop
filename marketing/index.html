What's in the box

Every tool your ops team asked for. Nothing they didn't.

Merxio replaces a dozen half-built tools with one platform that actually models how your business works — from a rep's field visit to the accountant's month-end statement.

Billing & invoicing

Invoices per order, running credit ledger, payment tracking that's fully orthogonal from delivery status. Aging buckets, overdue alerts, statements.

Inventory management

Real-time on-hand, reserve / commit / release semantics that survive concurrent orders, low-stock and out-of-stock crossings with in-app alerts, bulk upload from Excel.

Order lifecycle

Placed → accepted → packed → out-for-delivery → delivered — with edit-at-approval, over-credit routing to admin, and backorder splits for out-of-stock lines.

Field-sales analytics

Per-rep dashboards with 15+ metrics, district-wise revenue, leaderboards, monthly targets with category breakdown, and target-achievement drill-downs.

Rep + store CRM

Store assignments, GPS-geo-fenced visits (in-store & remote), credit-limit workflows with admin approval, and per-store recommended-products suggestions.

Achievements & gamification

Monthly goals with rewards, rep-facing progress boards, one-click claim + office redemption flow, and live target vs. actual across the leaderboard.

AI-assisted forecasts

Full audit trail

Deep-dive

Built for how distribution actually works.

Billing

A credit ledger that never disagrees with reality.

Every order, payment, and cancellation flows through the same ledger. Available credit is derived — never stored — so it can't drift. Over-credit orders route to admin with a full paper trail. Aging buckets, statement-of-accounts, and overdue notifications, out of the box.

  • Payments orthogonal to delivery — a delivered order can still be unpaid
  • Admin override on over-credit with explicit exposure tracking
  • Automated overdue notifications every 7 days per unpaid order

Inventory

Reserve, commit, release — under load.

Stock movements are atomic — two concurrent orders competing for the last 10 units will never both succeed. Backorders for out-of-stock lines auto-split off, and auto-promote to "ready-to-submit" the moment inventory arrives. Bulk upload reads any reasonable Excel format.

  • Waiting-for-stock orders auto-promote when inventory lands
  • Low-stock + out-of-stock in-app notifications on threshold-crossing
  • Full stock-history per variant with reason codes and actor names

FAQ

Answers to what everyone asks first.

Can I import my existing product catalogue and store list?

Yes. Merxio's bulk-upload accepts standard Excel workbooks — product name, category, subcategory, variants, prices, opening stock, reorder levels — and auto-creates the taxonomy it doesn't find. Same for stores. Most teams onboard 5,000+ SKUs in a single afternoon.

How does the free trial work?

14 days on the Growth plan, all features unlocked, no credit card required. Cancel anytime, or downgrade to Starter at the end. Your data stays for 60 days if you pause, so you can pick up where you left off.

Does Merxio work for industries other than pet-shop wholesale?

Yes — the underlying model (multi-role auth, products with variants, stores with credit, orders with a full lifecycle) works for FMCG, pharma, hardware, textiles, and any B2B distribution business with field reps. We started with pet-shop distributors but the platform is domain-agnostic.

Where is my data stored?

Managed plans run on MongoDB Atlas + Cloudflare R2 in the Asia-Pacific region by default. Bi-weekly encrypted archives ship to your chosen bucket on Growth and above. The Sovereign one-time plan runs entirely on your infrastructure — we never see your data.

Can I export my data if I leave?

Any time. Every list endpoint has a CSV export, and admins can trigger a full DB archive on demand — you download a tar.gz of every collection as JSONL. No lock-in.

How is the Sovereign one-time plan different?

You get the complete source code, deploy it on your own infrastructure, and never pay us monthly again. Year one includes support + updates; renewals are 20% of the license fee. Best for enterprises with data-residency mandates or teams that want zero vendor dependency.

Do you offer a discount for annual billing?

Yes — 20% off list price when you pay yearly, on any monthly plan.
